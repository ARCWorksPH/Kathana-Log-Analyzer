👋 Welcome to the Kathana Log Analyzer

In Kathana, export your chat log, then drop the .txt files here. We'll count your Rupiah, Prana and damage and show which session was the best.

⚠ All numbers are calculated only from the logs you load. Your in-game results may differ depending on play style, gear, buffs, map, party, and other game factors.

"Kills" are estimated from Prana rewards (the log has no kill line). "Active time" leaves out gaps of 2+ minutes with no fighting or loot.
